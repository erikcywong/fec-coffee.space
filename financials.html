A Complete Property Cycle, Told in Numbers

一部完整的地产周期，用数字讲述

Golden growth (FY2017–FY2019) → pandemic shock (FY2020–FY2021) → delivery boom and volatility (FY2022–FY2024) → consecutive losses and deleveraging (FY2025–FY2026). Revenue peaked at HK$10.2bn in FY2024; profit peaked at HK$1.71bn in FY2019 — the two peaks never coincided, which is the structural problem this report addresses.

黄金增长期（FY2017–FY2019）→ 疫情冲击（FY2020–FY2021）→ 交付大年与波动（FY2022–FY2024）→ 连续亏损与去杠杆（FY2025–FY2026）。营收峰值FY2024达102亿港元，盈利峰值FY2019达17.1亿——两个峰值从未同步出现，这正是本报告要解决的结构性问题。

Analyst Read分析师解读

The two peaks never met

两个峰值从未相遇

Revenue peaked in FY2024 while profit peaked five years earlier in FY2019. Property development revenue is recognized on completion, so P&L strength is a lagging, lumpy echo of past cycles — while costs, interest and impairments are current. This mismatch is why net margin collapsed from 27% (FY2018) to −18% (FY2026).

营收峰值出现在FY2024，盈利峰值却在五年前的FY2019。地产收入按竣工确认，损益表的强势是过去周期的滞后、脉冲式回声——而成本、利息与减值却是当期发生的。净利率从27%（FY2018）崩落至−18%（FY2026），根源正在于此。

Hotels: the undervalued stabilizer

酒店：被低估的稳定器

The hotel segment (~21% of FY2025 revenue) is the Group's largest recurring-income base and has been expanding asset-light (Dorsett management contracts, Singapore/Malaysia acquisitions with partner capital). It is the template for how a second recurring business — aeroponic coffee — should be built.

酒店分部（约占FY2025营收21%）是集团最大经常性收入来源，且正以轻资产模式扩张（帝盛管理合同、携伙伴资本收购新加坡/马来西亚项目）。这正是第二项经常性业务——雾培咖啡——应当复制的模板。

Adjusted cash profit is the tell

经调整现金溢利才是真相

Stripping fair-value noise, adjusted cash profit fell from HK$1.43bn (FY2022) to HK$266m (FY2025) and HK$109m (FY2026) — the underlying earnings engine is weakening faster than headline impairments suggest. Restoring it requires either a property upcycle or a genuinely new profit pool.

剔除公平值噪音后，经调整现金溢利从FY2022的14.25亿降至FY2025的2.66亿、FY2026的1.09亿港元——盈利引擎的弱化比表观减值所显示的更快。修复它，要么等待地产周期回暖，要么开辟真正的新利润池。
