Executive Summary核心结论

📉 Where the Group Stands: Deep Adjustment

📉 集团现状：深度调整期

FY2025–FY2026 losses were driven mainly by non-cash impairments and investment-property fair-value losses (~HK$1.2bn). The Group is monetising non-core assets (HK$2.5bn of transactions locked in FY2026) to cut debt; adjusted cash profit has thinned to HK$109m. The balance sheet is the battleground; the P&L follows the property cycle.

FY2025–FY2026亏损主因约12亿港元非现金减值与投资物业公平值损失。集团正出售非核心资产（FY2026锁定交易值约25亿港元）压降负债；经调整现金溢利已收窄至1.09亿港元。资产负债表是主战场，损益表跟随地产周期。

🧬 The Recommendation: US$30m, Three Phases

🧬 投资建议：三阶段合计3,000万美元

Deploy ≈HK$234m (US$30m) over five years into aeroponic coffee seedling farms and a tissue-culture lab — just 0.8% of adjusted NAV. Unit model: US$414k per farm, 615k seedlings/yr, US$2.26m revenue at a 73.5% gross margin, payback 2–3 years, IRR ≈25–35%. Failure of any phase is financially immaterial to the Group.

五年分期投入约2.34亿港元（US$3,000万）建设雾培咖啡幼苗农场与组培实验室——仅占经调整NAV约0.8%。单农场模型：投入US$41.4万，年产61.5万株，年收入US$226万，毛利率73.5%，回收期2–3年，IRR约25–35%。任何阶段失败对集团财务影响可忽略。

📊 The Payoff: Structural Re-rating

📊 回报：结构性重估

A 73.5%-margin recurring agri segment vs 24–27% cyclical property revenue; tissue-culture IP royalties; carbon-credit data assets; cheaper ESG-linked funding. At steady state the segment could lift blended gross margin by 3–5pct — and, more importantly, give the market a reason to close a >90% NAV discount.

73.5%毛利率的农业科技经常性收入 vs 24–27%的周期性地产收入；组培IP特许权收入；碳信用数据资产；更低的ESG挂钩融资成本。成熟期可将集团整体毛利率提升3–5个百分点——更重要的是，给市场一个收窄逾90% NAV折让的理由。
