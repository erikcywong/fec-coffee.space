From Cost Structure to Industrial Innovation

从成本结构到产业化创新

The NAPELL Aeroponic Coffee Cultivation System replaces traditional soil-based nurseries with a precision nutrient-mist pipeline: higher density, a 4-month growth cycle, three harvests per year, and year-round output under controlled climate — a data-driven commercial model for climate-resilient coffee seedling production.

NAPELL雾化水培咖啡种植系统以精密营养雾管道取代传统土壤苗圃：更高种植密度、4个月生长周期、年产3批、可控气候下全年产出——一个数据驱动、气候韧性的咖啡幼苗商业化生产模型。

The climate thesis

气候逻辑

ICO research projects global land suitable for coffee could contract by up to 50% by 2050 under climate stress, threatening the US$200bn+ coffee economy and 125 million livelihoods. Every displaced hectare creates seedling demand; every climate shock (El Niño 2026 among them) accelerates replanting with resilient, tissue-cultured stock. Demand here is driven by climate necessity — structurally uncorrelated with the property cycle.

ICO研究预测，气候压力下2050年全球适宜咖啡种植的土地面积可能缩减高达50%，威胁2,000亿美元以上的咖啡经济与1.25亿人生计。每一公顷退出的种植地都在创造幼苗需求；每一次气候冲击（包括2026年厄尔尼诺）都在加速抗逆组培种苗的补种。这里的需求由气候必要性驱动——与地产周期结构性负相关。

Why This Fits FEC's Asset Base为何契合远东发展的资产禀赋

Land in the right latitudes

恰在合适纬度的土地

FEC operates in Malaysia, Australia, Singapore and the UK — Malaysia and Queensland, Australia sit in or adjacent to viable coffee-belt latitudes. Idle or low-yield land parcels can be converted to greenhouses without new land acquisition, cutting the single largest capex item in the unit model.

远东发展业务覆盖马来西亚、澳洲、新加坡与英国——马来西亚与澳洲昆士兰正处于或毗邻可行的咖啡带纬度。闲置或低效地块可改造为温室而无需新购土地，直接削减单位模型中最大的资本开支项。

Hospitality as a demand anchor

酒店作为需求锚点

Dorsett's 50+ hotels provide an immediate captive demand channel: house-branded coffee, farm-to-lobby experiences at Queen's Wharf Brisbane, and agri-tourism add-ons. Zero customer-acquisition cost for the first tranche of output.

帝盛酒店50+家物业构成即时自有需求渠道：自有品牌咖啡、布里斯班Queen's Wharf的农场到大堂体验、农旅增值项目。首批产出零获客成本。

Proven green-finance rails

已验证的绿色融资通道

The Group has executed three sustainability-linked loans (latest HK$540m, 2024). A quantifiable carbon-data asset makes the aeroponic segment natural collateral for the next, cheaper generation of green instruments.

集团已落地三笔可持续发展贷款（最近一笔5.4亿港元，2024年）。可量化的碳数据资产使雾培分部成为下一笔、更便宜的绿色融资工具的天然抵押品。
